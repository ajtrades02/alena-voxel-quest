# Alena's Voxel Quest

A small 3D pixel-art (voxel) browser game. Help **Alena** explore a blocky island, collect all 12 spinning gems, dodge the red Grumble blocks and spikes, and reach the glowing portal to win. Alena has 3 hearts.

**Play:** https://ajtrades02.github.io/alena-voxel-quest/

## Controls
- **Keyboard:** WASD or arrow keys to move, Space to jump, Q / E or mouse drag to turn the camera, M to mute.
- **Phone / tablet:** drag on the left side to move (joystick), tap JUMP on the right, drag on the right side to turn the camera.

Tip: jump on a Grumble block's head to make it dizzy. Don't fall in the water!

Single self-contained `index.html` (three.js inlined), works offline, no network requests.
