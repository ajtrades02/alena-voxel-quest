# Alena's Voxel Quest

A small 3D pixel-art (voxel) browser game. Help **Alena** and her Yorkie puppy **Biscuit** explore a blocky island, collect all 12 spinning gems, dodge the red Grumble blocks and spikes, and reach the glowing portal to win. Alena has 3 hearts.

**Play:** https://ajtrades02.github.io/alena-voxel-quest/

## Vehicles
Hop on and ride around the island (Biscuit rides along too):
- **Range Rover** (white luxury SUV): fast, wide and steady, with exhaust smoke, an engine that revs, and a horn.
- **Harley Davidson** motorcycle: the fastest on land, and it leans into turns.
- **Bicycles** (two): slow and nimble, with a bell and a basket for Biscuit.
- **Rowboat**, **Bentley Speedboat** and **Jet Skis** (two): moored by the wooden dock. They only drive on water, and water can't hurt Alena while she rides.

While riding, spikes can't hurt her, Grumbles bounce away, and she can still collect gems.

## Controls
- **Keyboard:** WASD or arrow keys to move or steer, Space to jump, **E** to ride or get off, **H** to honk, Q / R or mouse drag to turn the camera, M to mute.
- **Phone / tablet:** drag on the left side to move or steer, tap JUMP, tap **RIDE** / **EXIT** near a vehicle, **HONK** while riding, and drag on the right side to turn the camera.

Tip: jump on a Grumble block's head to make it dizzy. Don't fall in the water on foot!

Single self-contained `index.html` (three.js inlined), works offline, no network requests. Blocky look-alike vehicles only; no real logos or trademark artwork.
