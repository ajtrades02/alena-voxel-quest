# Alena's Voxel Quest

A small 3D pixel-art (voxel) browser game. Help **Alena** and her Yorkie puppy **Biscuit** explore **Alena City**, a neon voxel Vegas on an island. Collect all 12 spinning gems, dodge the red Grumble blocks and spikes, and reach the glowing portal at the north end of the Strip to win. Alena has 3 hearts.

**Play:** https://ajtrades02.github.io/alena-voxel-quest/

## Alena City
- **The Strip:** a wide multi-lane boulevard with a palm-tree median, street lamps, and cross streets and avenues that loop around the city.
- **Landmarks** (original blocky look-alikes, no real logos): a pyramid with a light beam, a castle casino, a dancing-fountain lake in front of a resort tower, a mini tower of lights, a big observation wheel, and gold, glass-blue, white, pink, purple and teal towers.
- **Neon everywhere:** glowing tower edges, flashing marquee signs, and a "Welcome to Fabulous Alena City" diamond sign. Press **N** (or tap DUSK) to switch between day, dusk and night.
- Parked cars and taxis line the cross streets, and a wooden dock with boats sits at the south beach.

## Vehicles
Hop on and ride (Biscuit rides along too):
- **Range Rover** (white luxury SUV): fast, wide and steady, with exhaust smoke, an engine that revs, and a horn.
- **Harley Davidson** motorcycle: the fastest on land, and it leans into turns.
- **Bicycles** (two): slow and nimble, with a bell and a basket for Biscuit.
- **Rowboat**, **Bentley Speedboat** and **Jet Skis** (two): at the dock. They only drive on water, and water can't hurt Alena while she rides.

While riding, spikes can't hurt her, Grumbles bounce away, and she can still collect gems.

## Controls
- **Keyboard:** WASD or arrow keys to move or steer, Space to jump, **E** to ride or get off, **H** to honk, Q / R or mouse drag to turn the camera, N for day/dusk/night, M to mute.
- **Phone / tablet:** drag on the left side to move or steer, tap JUMP, tap **RIDE** / **EXIT** near a vehicle, **HONK** while riding, and drag on the right side to turn the camera.

Single self-contained `index.html` (three.js inlined), works offline, no network requests. Blocky look-alikes only; no real logos or trademark artwork.
